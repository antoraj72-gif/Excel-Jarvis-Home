Features

Your sales reports, done for you

Everything a sales team builds in Excel every month – without remembering a single formula.

Work Sheet that listens

Type or speak a request like “top 5 customers by sales value”. Excel Jarvis builds the sheet and the formulas – SUMIFS, VLOOKUP, pivots – for you.

Smart Suggestions

It reads your file and lists only the reports your columns can really make, from basic to advanced. Tick, preview, done.

Target vs Achievement

Achievement % for every salesperson with charts, low performers by ASM or TL, and only employees who are still working are counted.

Growth, GOLY and GOLM

Compare this year with last year, or the latest month with earlier months – dealer by dealer, in quantity or value.

Report from your sample

Upload your manager’s format – even a photo – and get the same report built from your data, with charts.

Export in one tap

Excel, Excel with formulas, PowerPoint, PDF or CSV – with your name and logo on the cover – then share on WhatsApp or email.

Safety

Your data stays with you

Excel Jarvis runs on your own device and is locked to it.

Works offline

After the first install, everything runs on your phone or laptop – no internet needed.

Nothing is uploaded

Your files never go to any cloud, AI service or to the app owner. They leave only when you tap Export or Share.

One device, one key

The app is locked. Each unlock key works only on the device it was made for, opened with your fingerprint or password.

No hidden code runs

Files are read as data only – Excel macros or anything hidden inside a file are never run.

Help

Questions people ask

How do I get Excel Jarvis?

Tap Install, then Set up this device with your fingerprint, screen lock or a password. Send the Device ID it shows to the person who shared Excel Jarvis with you, and paste the unlock key you get back. You do this only once on each device.

Can I use it on my phone and laptop?

Yes. Set up each device the same way and send its Device ID. Your laptop key ends on the same date as your phone.

Is my sales data safe?

Yes. Files are read and kept only on your own device. Nothing is sent to any cloud, AI server or to the app owner, and the app is blocked from sending data to any website.

Does it need internet?

Only the first time you open it, for software updates, and for voice input. Everything else works fully offline.

Can I type in Tamil?

Yes. Type in English, தமிழ் or Tanglish – it works offline. Voice input works too, using your phone’s speech service.

What happens when my access days are over?

The app asks for a new key. Send your Device ID again and you get a renewed key. Your saved work stays on your device.
